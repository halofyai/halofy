# Halofy Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0 — effective 2026-08-22
Contact: <licensing@halofy.ai>

This file contains two agreements. Sign the one that applies to you:

- **[Individual CLA](#individual-contributor-license-agreement)** — you are
  contributing on your own behalf.
- **[Corporate CLA](#corporate-contributor-license-agreement)** — your employer
  owns your work product, or you are contributing on behalf of a company or
  other legal entity.

If you are employed and your work is related to what you do for your employer,
you likely need **both**: the Corporate CLA signed by someone authorised to bind
your employer, and your own Individual CLA.

## Why this exists

Halofy is published under two licences: AGPL-3.0-or-later for the kernel and
console, Apache-2.0 for the driver interface, MCP surface and integrations.
Halofy also sells a commercial licence to organisations whose deployment cannot
accept the AGPL's network-copyleft obligation. That commercial licence is what
funds the open work.

Halofy can only offer those terms for code it has the right to sublicense. Every
contribution needs an explicit, written grant covering all three: AGPL,
Apache-2.0, and proprietary terms. That is what this agreement is.

**You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. You may
continue to use, publish, and relicense your own contribution anywhere else,
under any terms you like. Nothing here is exclusive.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot comments with a link and the exact sentence to post
as a comment. Signing takes about thirty seconds and is required once, not per
pull request.

Corporate CLAs are signed out of band — email <licensing@halofy.ai> with the
completed schedule below.
